Design the surround around a valid portal opening and a usable approach.
- Use the portal operation for the working frame and opening. Select a size that fits the request, including the two-by-three nether minimum when appropriate; do not enlarge it merely for spectacle.
- Choose one composition: a gate, sword, ruined arch, shrine or statue. Establish its full silhouette, bounds and support before ornamenting the border.
- Keep decoration outside the working frame and opening. A layered border can use slabs, stairs and related masonry to suggest thickness; crying obsidian can decorate but cannot replace required frame blocks.
- A sword can make the portal its blade, with a crossguard, grip and pommel sized for the available space. A ruin can use an interrupted outer border while retaining the complete working frame.
- Preserve two blocks of open floor in front of a nether portal and the space above an end portal. Join the approach to the threshold without a gap or decorative obstruction.
- Coordinate the ground, light and surrounding blocks with the portal's setting. Soul lanterns, glowstone or end rods can mark the entrance; never use forbidden fire or lava blocks.
- The portal is applied after other operations. Keep the rest of the design clear of the cells it owns.
