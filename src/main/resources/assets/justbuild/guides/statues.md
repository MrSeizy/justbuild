Resolve a statue's silhouette, pose and support before surface detail.
- Choose scale from the requested subject, viewing distance and site. Small statues can work with a few readable shapes; there is no universal minimum height.
- Minecraft mobs can use box-like fills in their recognizable proportions. Organic creatures can combine ellipsoids for torso, head and limbs, refined with smaller shapes at joints.
- Establish the line of action and points of contact. A standing figure needs grounded feet or an appropriate base; a flying or floating subject needs the support or suspension intended by the request.
- Mirror symmetric anatomy or paired features when useful. Deliberate asymmetry can make a pose readable; do not mirror a gesture that should differ across sides.
- Use voxels for a face, emblem or other small pattern after the main shape works. Eyes and markings need contrast at the intended viewing distance.
- Assign materials to major surfaces and a few accents. Restrained stone gradients or localized weathering can describe form; random mixing can obscure it.
- A plinth should support the pose and leave the statue prominent. Check the whole outline from several sides, including spaces between limbs and around any portal opening.
