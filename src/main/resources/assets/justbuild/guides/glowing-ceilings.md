Plan lighting around circulation, tasks and the requested mood.
- Place visible lights where they explain use: entrances, stair landings, work surfaces and gathering spaces. A ruin or dark scene may deliberately use fewer lights.
- Lanterns can hang directly from beams or short iron_chain runs. Place their supports first and check clearance below. Post lights belong beside paths, not in their walking width.
- Concealed ceiling lights can use sea lanterns, froglights, glowstone or shroomlight behind suitable trim. A top-half trapdoor below a light suggests a panel; choose a block and state that leave the intended effect visible.
- Light blocks under carpets, within vegetation or behind a recessed surface can soften their appearance. Preserve supporting floors and usable furniture access.
- Repeat lights in relation to structural bays or route changes. A fixed spacing does not guarantee a particular light level or prevent all hostile spawning; consider obstructions and the actual result.
- Candles, hearths and end rods offer different visual rhythms. Avoid covering every surface with a different light source.
